Who I Am

About Me

I'm a Data Analyst based in Lagos, Nigeria, with hands-on professional experience turning complex, messy data into clear stories that drive real business decisions.

My work spans the full analytics lifecycle — collecting and cleaning raw datasets, writing optimised SQL queries, building DAX-powered Power BI dashboards, and presenting findings to stakeholders in a way that's easy to understand and act on.
